# Phoneme Patrol

An adaptive phonics case study for ages 4–6, built around a simple idea: measure how *sure* a child is, not just whether they're right.

**Live demo:** https://chloenewell.github.io/phoneme-patrol/

## What's here

- The core confidence-check loop and why it exists
- How the three-way adaptive engine responds (reinforce quietly / skip ahead / catch the real misconception)
- The world-progression reward system, and why it beats a coin counter for this age group
- How the same engine scales across phonemes, blends, digraphs, sight words, and word families

## Standards & pedagogy

CCSS.ELA-Literacy.RF.K.2.D (Isolate & Blend CVC Phonemes) · Confidence-aware adaptive learning · Designed for Age of Learning / ABCmouse
